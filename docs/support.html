ROTATE - Gravity Match Support

Need help with ROTATE? Email rotate.benjaminsgames@gmail.com. Please describe your device, iOS version, and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open ROTATE's Store and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the Store cannot load, check your internet connection and try again.

Why is a purchase not appearing?

Allow a moment for the App Store to respond, then reopen the Store or tap RESTORE PURCHASES. If it is still missing, contact us with the product name and approximate purchase date. Do not email your full receipt or payment details.

Why am I out of Energy?

Energy refills over time. A finalized failed attempt or restarting an active attempt uses Energy. The Energy panel shows the current balance and refill timing. Starting a level normally does not use Energy.

What if the game looks wrong or a button does not respond?

Close and reopen the app, then check for an available update. If the problem persists, email the level number and a short description.
