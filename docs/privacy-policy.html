ROTATE - Gravity Match Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Publisher: Benjamin McCain

About this policy

This policy explains how ROTATE - Gravity Match ("ROTATE") handles information when you play the game, view ads, or make an in-app purchase. In this policy, "we" means the publisher named above.

Information stored on your device

ROTATE saves game progress in the app's local storage. This includes unlocked levels, best stars and scores, music and sound settings, Energy and its refill time, an ad-session counter, and a local record of purchase benefits such as Remove Ads, Premium, and the end time of a 24-hour Unlimited Energy period. This information lets the game resume your progress and show your available benefits. ROTATE does not provide an account or a game-operated cloud-save service. ROTATE's own game code does not send this local progress file to a ROTATE-operated server.

Your device's operating-system backup settings may separately affect whether app data is included in a device backup. That is controlled outside ROTATE.

Advertising

ROTATE uses Google AdMob to show ads. ROTATE requests Limited Ads, disables ad personalization and publisher first-party ID, and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. You may choose to watch rewarded ads for extra moves or Energy. The game also uses interstitial ads at certain points. A Remove Ads purchase suppresses forced interstitial ads; Premium does so as well. Optional rewarded ads remain available if you choose to use them.

Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process information associated with ad delivery and measurement, depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the ad configuration. Google's published iOS disclosure describes possible processing of IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The exact information processed in a particular session may vary. See Google's Mobile Ads data-disclosure guide and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising consent and choices

ROTATE uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent information and present a consent form when one is required. ROTATE checks whether Google permits ads to be requested before loading them. The consent choices available to you can vary by region and applicable rules. When Google requires a privacy-options form, you can use ROTATE's in-app Privacy Choices control to reopen it and review or change your choices. For more about Google's handling of advertising information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

ROTATE offers 24 hours of Unlimited Energy, Remove Ads, and Premium. Purchases and payments are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. ROTATE receives product and transaction status needed to provide and restore what you purchased, including a purchase date for the time-limited Energy benefit. ROTATE does not receive your payment-card number. Apple's handling of App Store and payment information is described in App Store & Privacy.

Retention and deletion

The local progress and settings file remains in ROTATE's app storage until it is removed through your device's app-data controls or the app is deleted, subject to any operating-system backup behavior. Removing local data does not cancel an Apple purchase or erase Apple's transaction history. Google and Apple maintain information under their own policies; ROTATE cannot state or change their retention periods here. To ask about ROTATE's own data practices, use the contact address below.

Children's privacy

ROTATE - Gravity Match is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Children may enjoy the game, but the app is not designed or marketed specifically for children under 13. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that requires parental consent under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the revised text at the public policy URL and update the effective date above.
